Why this is different

01

Sealed before the test

What will be tested, and the score it has to beat, are hashed and timestamped onto the Bitcoin blockchain before the data is touched. The story cannot be rewritten once the answer is known.

02

Luck is measured first

Test a thousand random strategies and the best will look brilliant by chance. Every result is scored against how good pure luck would have looked with the same number of tries.

03

Nothing is hidden

Failures, near misses, and runs that had to be thrown away all stay on the record with the reason attached. A registry that deletes its own mistakes is asking not to be believed.
